If a : b :: c : d then ad = bc (product of extremes = product of means).

CONTINUED PROPORTION a : b :: b : c gives b² = ac, and b is the mean proportional.

The four operations to know by name, because questions ask for them by name:
COMPONENDO: a/b = c/d → (a+b)/b = (c+d)/d
DIVIDENDO: a/b = c/d → (a−b)/b = (c−d)/d
COMPONENDO AND DIVIDENDO: a/b = c/d → (a+b)/(a−b) = (c+d)/(c−d)
ALTERNENDO: a/b = c/d → a/c = b/d

A. Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. (a) −1.

In a proportion, the product of the EXTREMES equals the product of the MEANS.
Extremes: (x + 3) and −5. Means: 1 and (3x − 7).

(x + 3)(−5) = 1 × (3x − 7)
−5x − 15 = 3x − 7
−5x − 3x = −7 + 15
−8x = 8
**x = −1**

CHECK: with x = −1 the four terms are 2, 1, −10, −5.
Product of extremes = 2 × (−5) = −10. Product of means = 1 × (−10) = −10. Equal ✓

Q2. If b is the mean proportional between a and c, then a : b :: b : c, so b² = ac.

b² = 9 × 25 = 225
b = √225 = **15**

(The positive value is taken, as is conventional for a mean proportional.)

CHECK: 9 : 15 :: 15 : 25, since 9 × 25 = 225 and 15 × 15 = 225 ✓

Q3. (a + b)/(a − b) = 7/3

Apply COMPONENDO AND DIVIDENDO in reverse — that is, add and subtract to recover a and b separately.

By componendo and dividendo applied to the fraction 7/3:
[(a + b) + (a − b)] / [(a + b) − (a − b)] = (7 + 3)/(7 − 3)

Simplify the left side:
(a + b + a − b)/(a + b − a + b) = 2a/2b = a/b

Simplify the right side:
10/4 = 5/2

Therefore a/b = 5/2, that is **a : b = 5 : 2**

CHECK: take a = 5, b = 2. Then (a+b) : (a−b) = 7 : 3 ✓

This is the standard use of componendo and dividendo, and recognising when to apply it — whenever a sum and a difference appear together — saves a great deal of algebra.

Q4. Apply COMPONENDO AND DIVIDENDO to x/1.

x/1 = [√(3a+2b) + √(3a−2b)] / [√(3a+2b) − √(3a−2b)]

(x + 1)/(x − 1) = [{√(3a+2b) + √(3a−2b)} + {√(3a+2b) − √(3a−2b)}] / [{√(3a+2b) + √(3a−2b)} − {√(3a+2b) − √(3a−2b)}]

The numerator becomes 2√(3a+2b) and the denominator 2√(3a−2b):
(x + 1)/(x − 1) = √(3a+2b) / √(3a−2b)

Square both sides:
(x + 1)²/(x − 1)² = (3a + 2b)/(3a − 2b)

Apply componendo and dividendo again:
[(x+1)² + (x−1)²] / [(x+1)² − (x−1)²] = [(3a+2b) + (3a−2b)] / [(3a+2b) − (3a−2b)]

Left side: (x² + 2x + 1 + x² − 2x + 1)/(x² + 2x + 1 − x² + 2x − 1) = (2x² + 2)/(4x)
Right side: 6a/4b

So (2x² + 2)/(4x) = 6a/(4b)

Cross-multiply:
4b(2x² + 2) = 4x × 6a
8bx² + 8b = 24ax

Divide throughout by 8:
bx² + b = 3ax

**bx² − 3ax + b = 0** — as required. ∎

THE PATTERN, which is worth memorising: componendo and dividendo, then SQUARE, then componendo and dividendo again. Almost every proof of this shape in the ICSE papers follows exactly those three steps.
