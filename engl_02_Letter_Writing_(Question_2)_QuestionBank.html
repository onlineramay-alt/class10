The syllabus states that 'the format of the letter with address, introduction, conclusion, etc. will form part of the assessment'. Format marks are therefore free marks and are lost every year by candidates who write a good letter in the wrong shape. Two complete model letters are given below — one formal, one informal — and the remaining answers give the plan and the specific content points, on the assumption that the format has been learnt from those two.
