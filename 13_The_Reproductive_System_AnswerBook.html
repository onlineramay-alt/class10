Q102. (a) Reproduction — the biological process by which living organisms produce new individuals of their own kind, ensuring continuity of the species.

Q103. (a) Spermatogenesis is the formation of sperms in the seminiferous tubules of the testis; Oogenesis is the formation of ova in the ovary.
(b) Implantation is the attachment/fixing of the developing blastocyst to the uterine wall; Gestation is the entire period of intra-uterine development of the foetus, from fertilization to birth.
(c) Pregnancy is the entire period during which a fertilized ovum develops into a baby inside the uterus; Parturition is specifically the process of childbirth, i.e. expulsion of the baby at the end of pregnancy.
(d) Placenta is the disc-shaped structure attached to the uterine wall through which the foetus exchanges nutrients/gases/wastes with the mother; the Umbilical cord is the cord-like structure connecting the foetus to the placenta, carrying the umbilical blood vessels.
(e) Identical twins develop from a single fertilized ovum (zygote) that splits into two, so they are genetically identical and always of the same sex; Fraternal twins develop from two separate ova fertilized by two different sperms, so they are genetically as different as ordinary siblings and may be of the same or different sex.
