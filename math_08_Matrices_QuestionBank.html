Mathematics — Matrices

ICSE Class 10 Question Bank

Generated 2026-09-10

The ICSE course covers only order, addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication and matrix multiplication of matrices up to 2×2. Determinants and inverses are NOT in the syllabus.

Multiplication rule: A(m×n) × B(n×p) is defined only when the inner numbers match, and the product has order m×p. Row of the first times column of the second. Matrix multiplication is NOT commutative — AB ≠ BA in general.
