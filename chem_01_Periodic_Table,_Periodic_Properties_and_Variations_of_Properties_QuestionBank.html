Chemistry — Periodic Table, Periodic Properties and Variations of Properties

ICSE Class 10 Question Bank

Generated 2026-09-10

The periodic trends are the whole of this chapter, and almost every question reduces to one of them. Learn the four — atomic size, ionisation energy, electron affinity, electronegativity — with the direction of change across a period and down a group, and the reason for each. Metallic character and non-metallic character follow from them.
