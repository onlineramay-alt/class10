THE FORMULAE. Take π = 22/7 unless told otherwise, and choose r and h so the sevens cancel.

CYLINDER: CSA = 2πrh TSA = 2πr(r + h) Volume = πr²h
CONE: CSA = πrl TSA = πr(r + l) Volume = (1/3)πr²h and l² = r² + h²
SPHERE: SA = 4πr² Volume = (4/3)πr³
HEMISPHERE: CSA = 2πr² TSA = 3πr² Volume = (2/3)πr³

NOTE the hemisphere: its TOTAL surface area is 3πr², not 2πr², because the flat circular face must be added.

In every 'melted and recast' problem, VOLUME IS CONSERVED — set the two volumes equal. Surface area is not conserved.

A. Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. (c) √3 : 2.

TSA of the sphere = 4πR²
TSA of the SOLID hemisphere = 3πr² (curved 2πr² plus the flat circular face πr²)

Setting them equal:
4πR² = 3πr²
4R² = 3r²
R²/r² = 3/4
R/r = √3/2

**R : r = √3 : 2**

The trap is using 2πr² for the hemisphere, which would give R : r = 1 : √2 — an answer not among the options, so the mistake is detectable. The word SOLID is what tells you to include the flat face.

Q2. (b) 1 : 2. Volume of a cone ∝ r²h. Ratio = (1² × 2) : (2² × 1) = 2 : 4 = 1 : 2.

Q3. (a) 3 : 4. Volume of a sphere ∝ r³, so (r₁/r₂)³ = 27/64 → r₁/r₂ = ∛(27/64) = 3/4.

Q4. (b) 2 : 7. Volume ∝ d²h (since r ∝ d). Ratio = (3² × 14) : (7² × 9) = 126 : 441 = 2 : 7.

Q5. (c) 4 cm. πr²h = 48π → r² × 3 = 48 → r² = 16 → r = 4.

Q6. (b) 7 cm. When a sector is folded into a cone, the radius of the sector becomes the SLANT HEIGHT of the cone. So the slant height = 7 cm. (The base radius would be 3.5 cm.)

Q7. (a) (A) is true, (R) is false. Cutting does not change the total volume, so (A) is true. But each hemisphere has surface area 3πr² (curved + flat face), so together 6πr², which is MORE than the sphere's 4πr² — (R) is false.

B. Worked Problems

Q8. SLANT HEIGHT, by Pythagoras:
l² = r² + h² = 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625
**l = 25 cm**

(7, 24, 25 is a Pythagorean triple, which is why these figures are chosen — recognising it saves time.)

CURVED SURFACE AREA:
CSA = πrl = (22/7) × 7 × 25 = 22 × 25 = **550 cm²**

TOTAL SURFACE AREA:
TSA = πr(r + l) = (22/7) × 7 × (7 + 25) = 22 × 32 = **704 cm²**

VOLUME:
V = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3) × (22/7) × 49 × 24
= (1/3) × 22 × 7 × 24
= (1/3) × 3696
= **1232 cm³**

CHECK the TSA independently: CSA + base area = 550 + πr² = 550 + (22/7)(49) = 550 + 154 = 704 ✓

Q9. When a solid is MELTED AND RECAST, the VOLUME IS UNCHANGED. That is the whole principle of the question.

Volume of sphere = volume of cylinder

(4/3)πR³ = πr²h

The π cancels from both sides:
(4/3)R³ = r²h

Substitute R = 7 and r = 7:
(4/3)(343) = 49h
1372/3 = 49h
h = 1372 / (3 × 49)
h = 1372/147
**h = 9.33 cm** (or 28/3 cm exactly)

CHECK by computing both volumes:
Sphere: (4/3)(22/7)(343) = 1437.33 cm³
Cylinder: (22/7)(49)(28/3) = 154 × 28/3 = 4312/3 = 1437.33 cm³ ✓

NOTE that π cancelled, so the value of π never mattered. Whenever both solids are being compared by volume, cancel π first — it saves arithmetic and removes a source of error.

Q10. VOLUME OF WATER:
V = πr²h = (22/7) × 7² × 10 = (22/7) × 49 × 10 = 22 × 7 × 10 = **1540 cm³**

TOTAL SURFACE AREA of the closed cylinder:
TSA = 2πr(r + h) = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × (7 + 10)
= 2 × 22 × 17
= **748 cm²**

For comparison, the CURVED surface area alone would be:
CSA = 2πrh = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 10 = **440 cm²**

READ THE QUESTION for which is wanted. A closed cylinder has TWO circular ends; an open one (a pipe, a vessel without a lid) has one or none. TSA = CSA + 2πr² for a closed cylinder, and CSA + πr² for one with a single open end.
