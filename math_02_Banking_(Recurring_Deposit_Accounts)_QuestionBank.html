The whole chapter rests on ONE formula, and everything else is rearrangement of it:

I = P × [n(n+1)/2] × (1/12) × (r/100)

where P is the monthly deposit, n the number of months and r the rate per annum. The factor n(n+1)/2 arises because the first instalment earns interest for n months, the second for (n−1), and so on down to 1 — and the sum of the first n natural numbers is n(n+1)/2.

Maturity value = total deposited (P × n) + interest.
