Generated 2026-09-10

SLOPE between two points: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)
SLOPE from an equation: rearrange to y = mx + c; the coefficient of x is the slope.
POINT–SLOPE FORM: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
SLOPE–INTERCEPT FORM: y = mx + c

PARALLEL lines have EQUAL slopes: m₁ = m₂
PERPENDICULAR lines have m₁ × m₂ = −1 (each slope is the negative reciprocal of the other)

A line parallel to the x-axis has slope 0 and equation y = c. A line parallel to the y-axis has an undefined slope and equation x = k.
