Biology — Diagram Questions: Draw vs. Label

Every diagram-related question across all 16 ICSE Class 10 Biology chapters, split into the two patterns the board actually uses: questions where you draw the diagram yourself, and questions where a diagram is printed on the paper and you label / identify / interpret it. Built 2026-09-27 by scanning the question bank's own source data (not a separate hand-picked list), so counts and sources are traceable back to the per-chapter Question Banks.

How to tell them apart in the exam: a DRAW question says “Draw a neat, labelled diagram of …” and gives you nothing to look at — you supply the picture from memory, usually for 3 marks. A GIVEN-diagram question says “Given below/alongside is a diagram of … Study the same and answer the questions that follow” — the picture is already printed on the paper, and you only label, identify, or interpret parts of it, usually as part of a bigger 4–10 mark structured question.

1. Draw-Your-Own-Diagram Questions

No image is given in the question. The student must produce the diagram from memory, usually as a short, standalone 3-mark item (or as the final sub-part of a larger structured question).
