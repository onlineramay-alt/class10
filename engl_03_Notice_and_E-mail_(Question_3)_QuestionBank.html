English Language — Notice and E-mail (Question 3)

ICSE Class 10 Question Bank

Generated 2026-09-01

This question was introduced in 2019 — the 2017 and 2018 papers have no Notice or E-mail question at all, and go straight from the letter to the comprehension. The two parts always share one situation: the notice announces the event, the e-mail does something about it (invites a judge, requests permission, asks a neighbouring school to send a team). Answer both from the same set of facts, and make sure the date, time and venue agree between them — examiners check.
