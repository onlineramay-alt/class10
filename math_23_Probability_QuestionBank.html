P(E) = (number of favourable outcomes) / (total number of possible outcomes)

0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1 always. P(impossible event) = 0. P(certain event) = 1. P(not E) = 1 − P(E).

The standard sample spaces:
A COIN: 2 outcomes. TWO COINS: 4. A DIE: 6. TWO DICE: 36.
A PACK OF CARDS: 52 — 26 red (13 hearts, 13 diamonds) and 26 black (13 spades, 13 clubs); 4 of each rank; 12 face cards (J, Q, K of each suit); aces are NOT face cards.
