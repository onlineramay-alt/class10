The only heavily numerical chapter of the course, and the calculations follow a small number of fixed routes. Every one of them goes through the MOLE: convert what you are given into moles, use the equation's coefficients to get moles of what you want, then convert back. The constants needed throughout: 1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles = the relative molecular mass in grams = 22.4 litres of any gas at STP.
