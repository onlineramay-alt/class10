THREE CONDITIONS FOR SIMILARITY: AA (two angles equal), SSS (three sides in proportion), SAS (two sides in proportion and the included angle equal).

THE RATIO THEOREM, which almost every question depends on. If two triangles are similar with sides in the ratio a : b, then

corresponding SIDES, ALTITUDES, MEDIANS and PERIMETERS are in the ratio a : b
corresponding AREAS are in the ratio a² : b²

So to go from an area ratio back to a length ratio, take the SQUARE ROOT.

A. Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. (a) 3 : 8.

For similar triangles, the ratio of AREAS is the SQUARE of the ratio of corresponding lengths — and an altitude is a length.

So if the ratio of altitudes is a : b, then
a² : b² = 9 : 64
a : b = √9 : √64
= **3 : 8**

Option (c) is the trap: it gives the area ratio unchanged, forgetting to take the square root. Option (d) reverses the order.

Q2. Since DE ∥ BC, the BASIC PROPORTIONALITY THEOREM (Thales' theorem) applies: a line drawn parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio.

AD/DB = AE/EC

3/5 = 4.5/EC

Cross-multiply:
3 × EC = 5 × 4.5
3 EC = 22.5
EC = 22.5/3
**EC = 7.5 cm**

CHECK the proportion: AD : DB = 3 : 5 = 0.6, and AE : EC = 4.5 : 7.5 = 0.6 ✓

WATCH THE PAIRING. The theorem pairs AD with DB and AE with EC — the two segments on each side, in the same order. Writing AD/AB = AE/EC would be wrong.

Q3. STEP 1 — establish similarity.
∠A = ∠D and ∠B = ∠E, so by the AA criterion, triangle ABC ~ triangle DEF.

STEP 2 — find EF using the ratio of corresponding sides.
Corresponding sides: AB ↔ DE and BC ↔ EF.
AB/DE = BC/EF
6/9 = 8/EF
6 EF = 72
**EF = 12 cm**

STEP 3 — find the area, using the SQUARE of the length ratio.
Ratio of sides = AB : DE = 6 : 9 = 2 : 3
Ratio of areas = 2² : 3² = 4 : 9

Area of ABC / Area of DEF = 4/9
24 / Area of DEF = 4/9
Area of DEF = 24 × 9/4
**Area of DEF = 54 cm²**

CHECK that the larger triangle has the larger area: DEF has the longer sides (9 and 12 against 6 and 8), and 54 > 24 ✓

Q4. STEP 1 — the ratio of PERIMETERS equals the ratio of corresponding SIDES (perimeter is a length).
Ratio = 30 : 45 = 2 : 3

STEP 2 — find the corresponding side.
12 / x = 2/3
2x = 36
**x = 18 cm**

STEP 3 — the ratio of AREAS is the SQUARE of the length ratio.
Ratio of areas = 2² : 3² = **4 : 9**

THE RULE IN ONE LINE: anything measured in ONE dimension — side, perimeter, altitude, median — is in the ratio a : b; anything measured in TWO dimensions — area — is in the ratio a² : b². (And a volume would be a³ : b³, which is worth knowing for the mensuration chapter.)
