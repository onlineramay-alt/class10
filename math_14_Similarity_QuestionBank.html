THREE CONDITIONS FOR SIMILARITY: AA (two angles equal), SSS (three sides in proportion), SAS (two sides in proportion and the included angle equal).

THE RATIO THEOREM, which almost every question depends on. If two triangles are similar with sides in the ratio a : b, then

corresponding SIDES, ALTITUDES, MEDIANS and PERIMETERS are in the ratio a : b
corresponding AREAS are in the ratio a² : b²

So to go from an area ratio back to a length ratio, take the SQUARE ROOT.
