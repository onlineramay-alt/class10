Mathematics — Measures of Central Tendency

ICSE Class 10 Answer Book

Generated 2026-09-10

MEAN of grouped data (direct method): mean = Σfx / Σf, where x is the class mark (the mid-value of the class).

MEDIAN of ungrouped data: arrange in order; if n is odd the median is the ((n+1)/2)th value; if n is even it is the mean of the (n/2)th and ((n/2)+1)th values.

MODE: the value occurring most often.

Always build the frequency table with columns for x, f, fx and cumulative frequency. The table earns marks even if the final arithmetic slips.

B. Worked Problems

Q6. Build the table.

x | f | fx
10 | 4 | 40
20 | 6 | 120
30 | 10 | 300
40 | 7 | 280
50 | 3 | 150
-----+-----+------
| 30 | 890

Σf = 4 + 6 + 10 + 7 + 3 = 30
Σfx = 40 + 120 + 300 + 280 + 150 = 890

Mean = Σfx / Σf = 890 / 30 = **29.67** (to two decimal places)

SANITY CHECK: the mean must lie between the smallest and largest values of x, that is between 10 and 50, and it should sit near where the frequencies are heaviest — the largest frequency is at x = 30, and 29.67 is close to 30 ✓

Q7. Add a CUMULATIVE FREQUENCY column.

x | f | cf
10 | 4 | 4
20 | 6 | 10
30 | 10 | 20
40 | 7 | 27
50 | 3 | 30

N = Σf = 30, which is EVEN.

For an even number of observations the median is the mean of the (N/2)th and the ((N/2) + 1)th values — that is, the 15th and 16th values.

From the cumulative frequency column: the values up to and including the 10th are 20, and the values from the 11th to the 20th are all 30.

So the 15th value = 30 and the 16th value = 30.

Median = (30 + 30)/2 = **30**

NOTE that mean (29.67), median (30) and mode (30, the value with the highest frequency) are all close here, which indicates a roughly symmetrical distribution. When they differ widely the distribution is skewed — a point examiners sometimes ask about.

Q8. First find the CLASS MARK of each class — the mid-value, given by (lower limit + upper limit)/2.

Class | class mark x | f | fx
0–10 | 5 | 5 | 25
10–20 | 15 | 8 | 120
20–30 | 25 | 12 | 300
30–40 | 35 | 7 | 245
40–50 | 45 | 3 | 135
----------+--------------+-----+-------
| | 35 | 825

Σf = 5 + 8 + 12 + 7 + 3 = 35
Σfx = 25 + 120 + 300 + 245 + 135 = 825

Mean = Σfx / Σf = 825 / 35 = **23.57** (to two decimal places)

THE ONE THING TO GET RIGHT: for grouped data you must use the CLASS MARK, not the class limits. Using 0, 10, 20, 30, 40 instead of 5, 15, 25, 35, 45 is the standard error and gives a mean about 5 too low.

SANITY CHECK: the mean lies between 0 and 50, and near the 20–30 class which holds the largest frequency ✓

Q9. Class marks (x): 4, 12, 20, 28, 36, 44. A = 28, class width h = 8.
u = (x − 28)/8: −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2.
fu: −30, −40, −14, 0, 18, 44.

Σf = 100, Σfu = −22.
Mean = A + (Σfu/Σf) × h = 28 + (−22/100) × 8 = 28 − 1.76 = **26.24**.
