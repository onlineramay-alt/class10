The whole chapter rests on ONE formula, and everything else is rearrangement of it:

I = P × [n(n+1)/2] × (1/12) × (r/100)

where P is the monthly deposit, n the number of months and r the rate per annum. The factor n(n+1)/2 arises because the first instalment earns interest for n months, the second for (n−1), and so on down to 1 — and the sum of the first n natural numbers is n(n+1)/2.

Maturity value = total deposited (P × n) + interest.

A. Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. (d) Both are the same, ₹18,000.

'Amount invested' means the total sum DEPOSITED — that is P × n — not the maturity value, and the rate of interest is irrelevant to it. That is what the question is testing.

A: P = 1,500, n = 12 months → 1,500 × 12 = ₹18,000
B: P = 1,200, n = 15 months → 1,200 × 15 = ₹18,000

Both invested **₹18,000**, so the answer is (d). The 9% is a distractor.

B. Worked Problems

Q2. GIVEN: P = ₹800, n = 18 months, r = 10% per annum.

STEP 1 — the equivalent principal for one month.
n(n+1)/2 = (18 × 19)/2 = 342/2 = 171

STEP 2 — the interest.
I = P × [n(n+1)/2] × (1/12) × (r/100)
I = 800 × 171 × (1/12) × (10/100)
I = 800 × 171 × 10 / 1200
I = 1,368,000 / 1200
I = **₹1,140**

STEP 3 — the maturity value.
Total deposited = P × n = 800 × 18 = ₹14,400
Maturity value = 14,400 + 1,140 = **₹15,540**

Always state both the interest and the maturity value separately — the question usually asks for both, and candidates who give only the maturity value lose a mark.

Q3. GIVEN: n = 3 years = 36 months, r = 8% per annum, I = ₹9,990. Find P.

STEP 1 — rearrange the formula for P.
I = P × [n(n+1)/2] × (1/12) × (r/100)

n(n+1)/2 = (36 × 37)/2 = 1332/2 = 666

9,990 = P × 666 × (1/12) × (8/100)
9,990 = P × 666 × 8 / 1200
9,990 = P × 5328 / 1200
9,990 = P × 4.44

STEP 2 — solve.
P = 9,990 ÷ 4.44 = **₹2,250**

STEP 3 — the maturity value.
Total deposited = 2,250 × 36 = ₹81,000
Maturity value = 81,000 + 9,990 = **₹90,990**

CHECK by substituting back: 2,250 × 666 × 8 ÷ 1200 = 9,990. ✓

Q4. GIVEN: P = ₹1,200, n = 2 years = 24 months, maturity value = ₹31,800. Find r.

STEP 1 — find the interest.
Total deposited = P × n = 1,200 × 24 = ₹28,800
Interest = maturity value − total deposited = 31,800 − 28,800 = ₹3,000

STEP 2 — substitute into the formula and solve for r.
n(n+1)/2 = (24 × 25)/2 = 600/2 = 300

I = P × [n(n+1)/2] × (1/12) × (r/100)
3,000 = 1,200 × 300 × (1/12) × (r/100)
3,000 = 1,200 × 300 × r / 1200
3,000 = 300r
r = 3,000 ÷ 300 = **10% per annum**

NOTE the shape of the three problems in this chapter: you are given three of the four quantities P, n, r, I and asked for the fourth. Always find the INTEREST first if you have been given the maturity value, because the formula is in terms of interest, not maturity value.
