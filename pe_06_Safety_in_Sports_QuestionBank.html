IMMEDIATE MANAGEMENT OF A SOFT-TISSUE INJURY — the R.I.C.E. procedure:
R — REST the injured part
I — ICE, applied for 15–20 minutes at a time
C — COMPRESSION with a crepe bandage
E — ELEVATION of the part above the level of the heart

And what NOT to do in the first 48 hours — H.A.R.M.: no Heat, no Alcohol, no Running, no Massage.
