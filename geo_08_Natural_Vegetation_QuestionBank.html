Natural Vegetation is Question 6 of Part II in every paper on record. Five types are on the syllabus — tropical evergreen, tropical deciduous, tropical desert, littoral (tidal) and mountain — and the questions turn on correlating each with its rainfall, its adaptations and its commercial value.
