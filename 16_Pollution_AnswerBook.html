D. Descriptive Type Questions

Q37. (a) Pollution: an undesirable change in the environment that harms living organisms (b) Waste: unwanted or discarded material from domestic, industrial or commercial activity (c) Air pollution: contamination of the atmosphere by harmful substances affecting living organisms (d) Oil spills: accidental release of petroleum into oceans/water bodies (e) Pesticides: chemical substances used to kill agricultural pests (f) Sanitary landfills: engineered depressions in the ground where waste is dumped and covered with a layer of soil daily (g) Noise: unpleasant, unwanted, and disturbing sound that interferes with hearing or communication

Official Answer

Q38. (a) Sewage is liquid domestic/community waste (mostly water with organic and inorganic matter); effluents are liquid waste discharged specifically from industries/factories. (b) Biodegradable waste is broken down by microorganisms (e.g. food waste); non-biodegradable waste is not broken down naturally and persists (e.g. plastic). (c) Smoke is a visible suspension of particulates/gases from burning; smog is a mixture of smoke and fog that reduces visibility and is more harmful to health.
